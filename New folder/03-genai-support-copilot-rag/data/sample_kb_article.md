# How to Reset Your Password and Secure Your Account

This article explains how to reset a forgotten password, what to do when reset
emails do not arrive, and how to secure an account after suspected unauthorized
access.

## Resetting a forgotten password

1. Go to the login page and click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter the email address associated with your account.
3. Check your inbox for a message titled "Reset your password" and click the
   secure link inside it.
4. Choose a new password with at least 12 characters, including one number and
   one symbol.
5. Log in with your new password.

## Password reset link expiry

Reset links expire **60 minutes** after they are issued, and each link can be
used only once. If the link has expired, request a new one from the login page;
older links become invalid as soon as a new request is made.

## I never received the reset email

- Check your spam or junk folder and any "Promotions" tabs.
- Confirm you used the exact email registered to the account; we send reset
  emails only to addresses that exist in our system (a security measure).
- Corporate firewalls sometimes block automated mail — ask your IT team to
  allow messages from `noreply@example.com`, then request the email again.
- Wait up to **10 minutes** before requesting another link; requests are
  rate-limited to one email every 2 minutes.

## Locked accounts

After **5 consecutive failed login attempts**, the account is locked for
**15 minutes** as a brute-force protection measure. The lock clears
automatically — no support ticket is needed. Repeated lockouts trigger a
security review email to the account owner.

## Securing your account after suspicious activity

If you received a password-reset email you did not request:

1. Do not click the link.
2. Log in directly and change your password from **Settings → Security**.
3. Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) under **Settings → Security → 2FA**.
4. Review **Settings → Sessions** and sign out of any unrecognized devices.

## Changing your email address

You can update your email under **Settings → Account → Email**. For security,
a confirmation link is sent to both the old and the new address; the change is
applied once the new address is confirmed within 24 hours.

## Still need help?

Contact human support via in-app chat or `support@example.com`. Include the
account email and a description of the steps you already tried so agents can
verify ownership faster.
