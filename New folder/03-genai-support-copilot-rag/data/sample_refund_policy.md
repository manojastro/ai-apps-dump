# Refunds and Cancellation Policy

This policy describes when refunds are available, how to request one, and how
long processing takes. It applies to all individual subscription plans purchased
through our website.

## Refund window

We offer a **30-day money-back guarantee** on all first-time subscriptions.
Requests submitted within 30 days of the original purchase date receive a full
refund, no questions asked.

## Non-refundable items

The following are not eligible for refunds:

- Renewal charges after the initial 30-day period (you may cancel renewal at
  any time to stop future billing).
- Annual plans canceled more than **14 days** after purchase; these are
  refunded **prorated** for unused whole months.
- Add-on credits that have already been consumed.
- Purchases made through third-party marketplaces — those follow the
  marketplace's own refund process.

## How to request a refund

1. Log in and open **Billing → Orders**.
2. Select the order and click **Request refund**.
3. Pick a reason and submit. You will receive a confirmation email immediately.

Alternatively, contact support with your order ID. Refund requests are
processed by the billing team within **1 business day** of submission.

## Processing time

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within
**5–10 business days**. Bank posting times vary; card refunds typically appear
in 3–5 business days, while some banks take the full 10. We cannot refund to a
different card or method than the one charged.

## Duplicate or accidental charges

Duplicate charges are always refunded in full regardless of when they are
reported. If you were charged twice for the same cycle, contact support with
both receipt numbers and mark the request urgent.

## Cancellations

Cancel anytime under **Settings → Subscription → Cancel**. Cancelling stops all
future renewals immediately; your plan remains active until the end of the paid
period. Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active
subscription — cancel first, then delete.

## Exceptions

Policy exceptions (e.g., refunds outside the standard window due to medical
emergency or service outage longer than 72 hours) require approval from a
support lead and are granted at company discretion, one exception per account.
