# 2026 job-market brief: Generative / Agentic / LLM / Applied AI engineering

**Access date:** 2026-09-15  
**Scope:** Generative AI Engineer, Agentic AI Engineer, LLM Engineer, AI Application Engineer, Applied AI Engineer, AI Platform Engineer. India first (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Gurugram, Noida, Mumbai, Kochi, Coimbatore, India remote), with a global comparison.  
**Method:** Live aggregator inventory (Naukri, LinkedIn, Glassdoor, AgenticCareers, Underprompt) plus official career pages and full or near-full job-description (JD) text for named employers. Skill frequencies are computed only on JDs whose text was actually inspected, not on headline search counts.

---

## 1. How to read this brief

Aggregator headline counts are **not** a census of unique open roles. Naukri’s “10,584 Generative AI Engineer jobs” and LinkedIn’s “11,000+ Generative AI Engineer jobs in India” mix exact titles, keyword-stuffed services reqs, duplicate Accenture/TCS postings, and adjacent AI/ML roles. The useful signal is **what full JDs actually require**.

This brief therefore separates:

1. **Market volume** (aggregator snapshots, inflated).
2. **Inspected JD sample** (N = 50 with extractable skills; 8 additional titles/locations noted but not skill-coded).
3. **Hype vs JD reality** (MCP, Google ADK, OpenAI Agents SDK, PydanticAI, A2A).
4. **Pay** (India bands only where a named source exists).

**Gaps (not invented):** Thoughtworks, Hugging Face, Mistral, Scale AI, Snowflake India, Oracle, Elastic, and IBM India did not yield inspectable full GenAI-engineer JDs on this date. Kochi and Coimbatore volume is thin. Most India JDs do not disclose CTC.

## 4. What the roles actually are

Three distinct jobs share overlapping titles:

| Cluster | Who hires | What the JD wants | Typical years |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Services / SI GenAI engineer** | TCS, Infosys, Accenture, Cognizant, Capgemini, PwC, Deloitte, CGI | Python + RAG + one agent framework + a hyperscaler (Azure OpenAI / Bedrock / Vertex). Client delivery, often hybrid PAN-India. | 4–10; TCS floor **5 yrs total IT** |
| **B. Product / GCC applied AI** | Google Cloud FDE, NVIDIA, Salesforce Agentforce, SAP, Databricks, Meta Partner Eng, MongoDB Gurugram | Strong SWE + RAG/agents + cloud-native. FDEs are customer-facing builders, not advisors. | 5–10+; Google FDE **8 yrs** |
| **C. Frontier applied / agent platform** | OpenAI ADE, Anthropic Applied/FDE, Cohere, Perplexity, Cognition | Ship production agents, MCP/tooling, evals, reliability. Frameworks optional; systems + judgment required. | 4–8 IC; staff+ higher |

**India services reality:** many “Architect” titles (especially Accenture “Large Language Model Architect”) are **staffing templates**. Some copies still say “design neural network parameters trained on unlabeled text” with **Good to have: NA**. Adjacent postings for the same title require Python, FastAPI, LangGraph, RAG, Docker/K8s. Treat Accenture raw counts as volume, not as 2,000 unique architecture seats.

**Forward Deployed / Applied AI** is the 2026 growth title at labs and clouds (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Cloud, Cognition, PwC “embedded in the client team”). It is **customer-facing software engineering**, not pre-sales only.

## 6. Hype vs JD reality

### MCP is real, not universal

MCP appears as **must-have** at TCS Hyderabad (“Agents development, A2A, MCP, Langgraph, Pydantic, Python, FAST APIs, LLM, RAG, AWS/Azure/GCP”), PwC Acceleration Centers, SAP Bangalore, Platlap PAN-India, OpenAI Agent Enablement, GRAIL, G-Research, Workfall. It is **preferred** at Google Cloud FDE and Atos ServiceNow FDE.

It does **not** appear in most Cognizant/Infosys/Deloitte/Siemens/WSP/NVIDIA RAG JDs inspected. AgenticCareers’ role definition now treats MCP as part of the agent-engineer identity, which **overstates** how often India SI JDs require it.

**Practical read:** MCP is a 2026 differentiator for agent-platform and FDE work. Listing it as a core skill is justified; treating it as table stakes for every “GenAI Engineer” in Bengaluru is not.

### A2A is still a TCS/ServiceNow-adjacent keyword

Only TCS (required) and Atos ServiceNow (advantage) in this sample. Do not build a curriculum around A2A based on market frequency.

### LangGraph beat “LangChain-only”

2024 JDs said LangChain. 2026 must-have lists say **LangGraph** (stateful graphs) and treat LangChain as the umbrella. CrewAI and AutoGen are almost always “or similar,” not exclusive. Semantic Kernel clusters with Azure. OpenAI Agents SDK and Google ADK are **vendor-native** and rare outside that vendor’s ecosystem plus a few sophisticated SIs (PwC AC lists OpenAI Agents SDK as mandatory).

### PydanticAI is a niche production taste, not a hiring filter

Three JDs (RoundCircle, Titan, G-Research). Pydantic (models/validation) is more useful than PydanticAI brand knowledge.

### RAG is still the job; “agents” is the label

Even JDs titled Agentic AI Engineer spend equal or more space on RAG, embeddings, vector search, APIs, and cloud. Multi-agent orchestration is required for Cognizant, PwC SA, Infosys, RoundCircle, SAP, Google FDE (preferred). Many “agent” JDs would be satisfied by a tool-calling RAG service with retries.

### Eval is the split between serious and keyword JDs

Google FDE, OpenAI ADE, Anthropic Agents, Baselayer, and Cognizant’s official Bangalore JD require evaluation (golden sets, hallucination, grounding). A large share of Naukri SI postings never mention eval, observability, or cost-per-token. That is the quality split, not LangChain vs LangGraph.

### Kubernetes is a platform/senior filter

K8s is required in PwC AC, Cognizant Agentic, Meta Partner, GRAIL. Mid-level India GenAI JDs more often stop at Docker + a managed cloud AI service (Azure OpenAI, Bedrock, Databricks). Underprompt’s “top skill: Kubernetes” reflects global ML infra postings, not India GenAI app JDs.

### FastAPI is over-taught relative to JD naming, under-taught relative to the actual job

Only ~10/50 name FastAPI. ~38/50 require shipping HTTP APIs. The skill is backend engineering, not the FastAPI brand.

### Accenture volume is not Accenture demand for LLM architects

Naukri shows **2,283** Accenture jobs under this search. Inspected copies include (a) empty templates (“Must have: LLMs”, “Good to have: NA”, 3 years) and (b) a minority of serious Python/LangGraph/RAG/K8s JDs. LinkedIn Hyderabad is flooded with duplicate “Large Language Model Architect” cards.

### Customer-facing is the hidden required skill for the best-paid cluster

OpenAI ADE, Google FDE, Meta Partner, NVIDIA SA, PwC embedded, Deloitte pre-sales, Cognition Applied, Cohere Applied: these JDs require leading technical discovery, writing code in the customer environment, and feeding product. India SI “collaborate with stakeholders” is weaker but still present.

## 9. Implications for a skills profile (evidence-based)

If the goal is to match **what JDs require in India plus stay portable to global Applied/FDE roles**:

1. **Python production engineering** (APIs, testing, Docker, CI/CD) — 49/50.
2. **RAG done properly** (chunking, hybrid retrieval, a vector store, grounding) — 42/50 mention RAG.
3. **One agent runtime in depth: LangGraph**, plus literacy in tool calling, not a zoo of frameworks — 24/50 mention LangGraph; CrewAI/AutoGen are OR-list padding.
4. **One hyperscaler AI stack** (Azure OpenAI *or* Bedrock *or* Vertex) plus provider-agnostic fallbacks (OpenAI + Claude).
5. **MCP as a differentiator**, not a substitute for RAG — required on the better 2026 agent JDs, absent on most SI spam.
6. **Eval + tracing + cost** — the quality bar that Google FDE / OpenAI ADE / PwC AC / Cognizant official already write down.
7. **Customer-facing delivery** if targeting FDE/Applied/consulting (Google, OpenAI, PwC, Deloitte, Meta, NVIDIA SA).
8. **Do not over-weight** Google ADK, OpenAI Agents SDK, PydanticAI, A2A, Qdrant, or Kubernetes unless the target employer is in that cluster.

## 10. Gaps and limitations

- Naukri blocked direct JD URLs from this environment; several India JDs were read from listing cards, LinkedIn public pages, and third-party mirrors. Where only a card was available, that is noted.
- LinkedIn public pages sometimes redirect expired JDs; Infosys and WSP were captured from search snippets + earlier crawl text, then cross-checked.
- **N = 50** is a convenience sample of named employers and live postings, not a random 1% of 10,584 Naukri rows.
- Thoughtworks, Hugging Face, Mistral, Scale AI, Snowflake India, Oracle, Elastic, IBM India, Wipro, KPMG: insufficient full JD text.
- Salary: no large *Generative AI Engineer*-only India compensation survey with public microdata. foundit and OYC are the best dated sources; Glassdoor title pages are weak.
- Official career-page JDs at OpenAI/Anthropic/NVIDIA/SAP/Salesforce/Cognizant/Google (via Jobaaj mirror) are higher fidelity than Naukri recruiter dumps.
