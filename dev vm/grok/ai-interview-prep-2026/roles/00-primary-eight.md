## Folding rules

- **LLM Engineer** (serving, KV cache, batching) is a track inside **GenAI / Agentic**. Use the LLM column in Chapter 03 if the JD is inference-heavy.
- **Forward Deployed AI Engineer** = FDE loop + RAG/agents/evals/HITL from the GenAI/Agentic set.
- **AI Deployment / GenAI Ops** = FDE ops side + Cloud + LLMOps. Use Chapters 16, 15, 17, 22.

## How to choose in 10 minutes

1. Read the JD verbs: build, deploy, advise, secure, automate, own on-call.
2. Match the object: RAG, agents, Bedrock, ServiceNow, MCP, Guardrails.
3. Pick the row above whose “actually hire you to do” sentence you can defend with a project.
4. If two rows fit, the **customer-facing** one (FDE / Solutions) beats the **product** one (GenAI) when the JD mentions travel, accounts, or “on-site.”
5. Do not prepare all eight. Weakness on your primary core-5 cells fails the loop.
