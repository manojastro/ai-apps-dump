# How to Access SAP

## Eligibility
SAP access needs a business justification and manager approval. The
application owner reviews entitlements every quarter.

## How to Request
1. Open the access request form on the IT portal and select the SAP role.
2. Attach manager approval and submit.
3. IT provisions access within 3 business days and notifies you.

## Support
SAP Basis support handles login and role errors via IT tickets.
