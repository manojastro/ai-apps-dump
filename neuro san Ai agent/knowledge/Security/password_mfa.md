# Password and MFA Standard

## Passwords
Use at least 14 characters with a manager-generated secret. Never reuse
corporate passwords on external sites. Change immediately if exposed.

## MFA
MFA is mandatory for VPN, email, and admin consoles. Lost MFA devices
must be reported to IT, which re-enrolls after identity verification.

## Lockout
Five failed sign-ins lock the account for 15 minutes. Contact IT to
unlock sooner with manager confirmation.
