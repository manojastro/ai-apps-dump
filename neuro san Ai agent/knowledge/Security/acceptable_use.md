# Acceptable Use Policy

## Scope
Corporate devices and accounts are provided for business use. Limited
personal use is tolerated where it does not risk security or productivity.

## Prohibited
Disabling endpoint protection, sharing credentials, and installing
unapproved remote-access tools are prohibited and may trigger access
revocation. Security owns enforcement.

## Reporting
Report phishing and lost devices to Security immediately via an IT ticket.
