# Internet Reimbursement Policy

## Eligibility
Employees working from home at least 3 days per week may claim up to
50 USD per month for home internet charges. Finance owns this policy.

## How to Claim
1. Pay your monthly internet bill and keep the receipt.
2. File an expense report in SAP Concur under category Telecom/Internet.
3. Attach the receipt and submit for manager approval.
4. Finance reimburses with the next payroll cycle after approval.

## Exclusions
Installation fees, hardware purchases, and mobile data plans are excluded.
Claims older than 90 days are not reimbursed.
