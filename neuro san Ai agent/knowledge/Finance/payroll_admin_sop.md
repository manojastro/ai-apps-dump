# Payroll Administration SOP

## Payroll Run
Payroll runs on the 25th of each month. Only payroll_admin staff may
execute or adjust a payroll run. All adjustments need dual approval.

## Employee Records Access
Individual salary, bank, and tax records are restricted to payroll_admin.
Never share another employee's payroll information with requesters,
including managers. Redirect such requests to HR with a ticket.

## PII Handling
Export payroll data only to the encrypted Finance share. Redact account
numbers in every report and ticket.
