# Travel Reimbursement Process

## Before You Travel
Get written manager approval and book through the corporate travel desk.
Per-diem rates follow the Finance travel table by city tier.

## How to Claim
1. Collect itemized receipts for transport, lodging, and meals.
2. File the trip expense report in SAP Concur within 14 days of return.
3. Attach approvals and submit to Finance for audit and payout.

## Exclusions
Alcohol, minibar, personal entertainment, and traffic fines are excluded.
