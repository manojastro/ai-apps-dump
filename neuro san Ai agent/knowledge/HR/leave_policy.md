# Leave Policy

## Annual Leave
Every employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Unused leave up to 5 days carries forward to Q1 of the next year.

## Sick Leave
Up to 12 paid sick days per year. A medical certificate is required for
absences longer than 2 consecutive days.

## How to Apply
Submit leave requests in the HR portal at least 3 business days in advance.
Your manager approves within 2 business days. HR is the policy owner.
