# VPN Help

## After a Password Change
Changing your password invalidates cached VPN tokens. Reconnect the VPN
client and sign in again with the new password to refresh the token.
The Zscaler client picks up the fresh token automatically on reconnect.

## Still Failing
1. Disconnect and reconnect the VPN client once.
2. Confirm the account is not locked and MFA passes.
3. Raise an IT ticket quoting the exact client error text.
