# New starter has no SAP access on day one

## Symptoms

A new starter can log into Windows but SAP shows 'no authorisation'.

## Cause

The HR feed created the AD account but the SAP role mapping job runs only at night.

## Workaround

Assign the SAP base role manually in SU01 for day one; the nightly job reconciles after.

## Permanent fix

Move SAP provisioning to the hourly JML micro-batch.
