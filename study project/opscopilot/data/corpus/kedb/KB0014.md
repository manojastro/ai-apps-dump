# Badge reader denies entry with green blink pattern 3-1

**Error code:** `BDG-31`

## Symptoms

The stockroom badge reader blinks green 3-1 and denies entry for a valid badge.

## Cause

The badge lost its stockroom access group after a department transfer.

## Workaround

Re-add the stockroom group in the badge portal and ask the user to tap twice after 5 minutes.

## Permanent fix

Automate group updates from the HR transfer feed.
