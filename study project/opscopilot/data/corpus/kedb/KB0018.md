# WiFi captive portal loops on customer tablets

## Symptoms

Customer tablets loop on the guest portal accept page without getting online.

## Cause

The portal cookie domain does not match the new guest gateway hostname.

## Workaround

Clear the tablet browser cache, open `http://portal.acme.local`, accept, and re-authenticate.

## Permanent fix

Fix the portal certificate SAN to include the new gateway name.
