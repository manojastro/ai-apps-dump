# Till screen freezes at 'Register closed' overnight

## Symptoms

The till app freezes on 'Register closed' and needs a hard reboot each morning.

## Cause

The overnight AV scan locks the till SQLite journal at 02:00.

## Workaround

Exclude `C:\AcmeTill\data\*.db-journal` from AV, restart the till service.

## Permanent fix

Move the AV window to 04:00 outside the close-of-day job.
