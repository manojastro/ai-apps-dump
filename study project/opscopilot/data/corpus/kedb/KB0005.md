# AD account locks after three bad logins at POS

## Symptoms

Cashiers get locked out after exactly three wrong PIN attempts at the POS login.

## Cause

The Acme POS account lockout policy is set to 3 attempts with a 15-minute reset window.

## Workaround

Unlock in AD Users and Computers, confirm the cashier uses the current 6-digit PIN, wait 15 minutes if it relocks.

## Permanent fix

Move POS logins to the self-service PIN reset kiosk to cut service-desk unlock tickets.
