# Laptop fan at 100 percent after VPN connect

## Symptoms

CPU fan spins at full speed within a minute of connecting the VPN.

## Cause

The old VPN client 5.1 polls the firewall every 200 ms when DPD is on.

## Workaround

Upgrade to VPN client 5.4 and set dead-peer-detection to 10 seconds.

## Permanent fix

Block client versions below 5.4 at the gateway.
