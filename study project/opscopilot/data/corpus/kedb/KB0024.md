# VPN drops every 60 minutes on the dot

## Symptoms

The VPN disconnects exactly every 60 minutes and reconnects cleanly.

## Cause

The gateway SA lifetime is 60 minutes and rekey fails on double-NAT home routers.

## Workaround

Set the client rekey margin to 5 minutes and enable keepalives every 30 seconds.

## Permanent fix

Raise the gateway SA lifetime to 8 hours in the next change window.
