# POS end-of-day reconciliation

## Purpose

Close each till and reconcile card totals before midnight.

## Steps

1. Run 'Close register' on every till.
2. Compare the Z-report card total to the estate manager.
3. If they differ by more than 1.00, recount cash and reprint.
4. File the Z-report in the safe log.

## Escalation

If the till freezes at 'Register closed', follow KB0022, then escalate to Store Systems.
