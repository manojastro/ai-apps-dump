# Replace till-area printer toner safely

## Purpose

Replace toner without dirtying stock or triggering the fire alarm.

## Steps

1. Put the printer in maintenance mode.
2. Remove the old cartridge into the bag.
3. Shake the new cartridge 5 times, remove the seal tape.
4. Insert, print a test page, log the swap.

## Escalation

If pages print blank after a swap, follow KB0028 before logging a hardware call.
