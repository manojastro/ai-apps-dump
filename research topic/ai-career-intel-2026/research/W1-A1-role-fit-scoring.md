## Method and rubric

### What this document is

A **role-fit scoring product** for one person. It is not a placement brochure. It does **not** pick “Cloud AI Engineer” as Primary because an older 2026 synthesis did. Every role is re-scored against **this** profile’s constraints: 8.5 YOE service-desk/IT ops as the qualification, Java/SQL-first refusal, ₹11 LPA floor, Coimbatore base, India metros + Remote India.

### Formula (non-negotiable)

Every role is scored 0–100 as a weighted sum of seven criteria, each itself 0–100.

```
TOTAL = 0.25*Demand
      + 0.20*Transferability
      + 0.15*Salary
      + 0.15*Growth
      + 0.10*LearningEase
      + 0.10*LowCompetition
      + 0.05*RemoteGeo
```

Round to nearest integer. Arithmetic is shown per role.

| Criterion | Weight | 100 means | 0 means |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Demand** | **25** | Dense live India JDs in Aug 2026 for 6–12 YOE in target cities; title is a real occupation, not keyword stuffing | Almost no honest India openings; title is marketing only |
| **Transferability** | **20** | 8.5 YOE service-desk / IT ops / ITSM / incident / troubleshooting is treated as a **qualification** in live JDs (ITIL, ITSM, on-call, runbooks, observability) | JDs require years of model training, CUDA, research, or Java/Spring core |
| **Salary** | **15** | Realistic first-switch cash CTC from ₹11 LPA with evidence (AmbitionBox n, disclosed JD bands). Stretch product/FAANG does **not** inflate this if unreachable | Pay-cut junior IC (₹6–11 L) or no salary evidence |
| **Growth** | **15** | 18–36 month path to senior applied AI / GCC without rewriting identity; K-curve right side | Dead-end (prompt intern, L1 AI trainer, classic RPA L2) |
| **Learning ease** | **10** | 90-day reachable with 12–15 hrs/week from an ops-lead job; Python+RAG/agents **or** ITSM+Copilot, not DSA+transformer math | Requires 12+ months CS/ML rewrite |
| **Low competition** | **10** | Fewer backend-Python/ML applicants; ops domain is a filter; Naukri/LinkedIn applicant counts if available | 200+ applicants, junior flood, tutorial-clone war |
| **Remote / geo fit** | **5** | Hybrid Coimbatore / Chennai / Hyd / true Remote India without a 5-day Bengaluru mandate | Onsite Bengaluru only, US-shift, 5-day TCS-style |
