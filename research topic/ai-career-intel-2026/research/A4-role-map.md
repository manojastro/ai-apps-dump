## Honest positioning doctrine (ethics + ATS)

### The legal / ethical line

**Do not write, imply, or ATS-stuff “8.5 years of GenAI / LLM / AI engineering.”** That is a false statement of fact. Indian recruiters and GCCs routinely ask for **total IT years + relevant GenAI years**. Cognizant itself publishes both numbers on the same line.

**INFERENCE:** “Related” and “relevant” are the words recruiters will probe. If the résumé says 8.5 years enterprise operations **and** 6–12 months of shipped GenAI artefacts (public repos, eval numbers, ServiceNow/Copilot PoCs), the Cognizant/Infosys/Accenture formula is still reachable **without lying**, because they already split total vs relevant.
