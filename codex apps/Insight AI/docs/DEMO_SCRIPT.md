# Five-minute demo

Preparation: start the local stack, sign in, and keep a completed evaluation handy. A 48-case benchmark can take several minutes, depending on process startup and machine load.

**0:00–0:40 — Boundaries.** Open Overview. Point out the visible replay label, persisted incident count, fixture integrations and independent worker heartbeat. Explain that this is scripted behavior, not a live-model quality claim.

**0:40–1:40 — Investigate.** Create a payment-delay replay incident. Watch the timeline. Open metrics evidence and the corroborating trace. Show their IDs, source records, timestamps and content hashes. Explain the difference between observations and inference.

**1:40–2:30 — Review and verify.** Open the exact recovery proposal. Show target, parameters, preconditions, expiry and hash. Approve it and inspect post-reset scripted samples. Explain that a successful executor response alone does not prove recovery. Separately show a rejected proposal remaining unexecuted.

**2:30–3:10 — Abstain.** Open an incomplete-evidence incident. Show the inconclusive explanation and missing information. No recovery action is proposed.

**3:10–4:20 — Evaluate.** Open the fixed-script versus regressed candidate report. Show the four pool-exhaustion regressions, expected outcome held by the controller, intermediate tool outputs and one-case reproducer. Explain that reviewed claim support and real-model evaluations remain missing.

**4:20–5:00 — Evidence and limits.** Export reports. Open the actual demo measurement JSON: all four faults were exercised locally, but some recovery samples were noisy. Show the missing-provider error and discuss what is still required for a live deployment.

Do not claim business savings, universal safety, human benchmark review, real-model success, or a production deployment.
